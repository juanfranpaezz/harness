# Entity-reading discipline: on-demand method

> A METHOD, not a tool or a build. An analyzer, the secretary or the orchestrator applies it **when a real entity has to be read**: a person, company, institution or market. It is NOT always-loaded text: load it on demand, at the trigger "I have to read X".

## The honesty floor (inviolable: reading people "like a human" relaxes the oracle, NOT this)
The output is ALWAYS a **probabilistic stance with confidence <100** ("~75%, conf 55; class X + 2 aggravators"), **NEVER** a verdict of intent ("X is lying / is corrupt / is deceiving us"). Reading the hidden why like a fortune-teller is cheating. What you deliver is a graded reading and its reasons, not a sentence.

## Inputs and output
- **Input: evidence packs.** Cited primary material about the entity: what it did (hires, spending, prices, filings, products shipped, who it partners with) and what it said (announcements, rules, judging criteria, interviews). Each item keeps its source and date.
- **Output 1: a model of what the entity wants.** Each objective with the evidence behind it, the channel it was read from (costly action or cheap talk) and a confidence below 100.
- **Output 2: the alignment vector.** A list of checkable things your proposal or deliverable must SHOW to line up with those objectives, each tied to the objective it serves. It is the hand-off to whoever builds or decides next.

## The method (in order)
> **Validated core (2 backtests on public cases: the US Federal Reserve in 2021 and RIM/BlackBerry in 2011, conf 74):** steps **1/2/4/5 are the ones that CARRY** (mandatory); step **3 reduces to one question**; step **6 is conditional**. Replicated on 2 entity types (institution + company).

1. **Outside view first (reference class).** Before looking at X's specifics, find the historical class it belongs to ("founder-CEO who just missed guidance", "currency under capital flight", "movement in its month 3") and start from what that class USUALLY does; only then adjust for the specifics. The base rate is the anti-bullshit anchor.
2. **Watch the feet, not the mouth (revealed preference).** Weigh what X DOES with costly/observable actions (who it hires/fires, where it puts capital, which insiders buy/sell) above what it SAYS. The costly channel is harder to fake; if there is only cheap talk, lower the confidence.
3. **(SECONDARY: one question, not an apparatus) Where do the frames disagree?** In one line: does the economic reading clash with the political/psychological one? (rational economically but cornered politically = interesting). **The full sweep of 5 separate frames is NOT needed**: in the 2 backtests that was mostly re-derivable from steps 1+2. One question, not a process.
4. **Pre-mortem (the home of the deception tell).** Ask "it is 12 months later and this reading was catastrophically wrong / X deceived me successfully: narrate how", and read the present hunting for the early signs of that story. It generates DISCONFIRMING readings on purpose.
5. **Panel of credible voices (anchored to real sources with OPPOSITE incentives).** Read X as 3-5 great named readers would (an adversarial short-seller, a veteran diplomat reading face/incentives, a political operator reading coalitions), each voice anchored to a REAL cited corpus with opposite incentives (not the model in disguise: that would be a correlated error). Agreement between different schools raises confidence; division lowers it and is reported as division.
6. **(CONDITIONAL: only if the entity is non-Western or its communication is performative/managed) Non-Western lens.** In the 2 backtests (both Western) this step was filler, so **do NOT run it by default.** It TURNS ON when the entity is non-Western or performative: import a real apparatus, e.g. Chinese (yuqing = governable public temperature; guanxi/face = obligations behind the stated reason), Russian (political technology: "whose hand does this mood serve?"), Indian (caste/patron-client), Japanese (periodic emotion). You never assert the foreign frame is TRUE; it is a lens the Western default omits. Gated primary sources (CNKI, eLibrary.ru) go to the investigator. **Honest caveat:** it was never tested where it SHOULD pay (the 2 tests were Western).

## When to use it / when NOT
- **YES:** any moment when an analyzer, the secretary or the orchestrator must read a real entity or motive under ambiguity.
- **Do NOT turn it into a ritual.** If nobody is applying it to a concrete entity, do not load it just in case. It lives at the real trigger.
- **Gates that still hold:** stating the motive of a LIVE entity to anyone outside is an outward action and needs the user's explicit go; readings of markets or crowds are observe-only (calibrate, never bet or publish).
